Screen Time access

Screen Time access won't turn on

iOS needs a few things before it allows access:

  • A device passcode, with Face ID or Touch ID set up.
  • An iCloud account signed in on this iPhone.
  • An internet connection for the first request.
  • No other parental controls app already authorized on this iPhone. If one is, remove its access first.
  • Screen Time not restricted, for example by a parent or work profile.

Then try again from Settings in ScreenSpan.

How do I turn off access?

On your iPhone, open Settings › Screen Time and remove ScreenSpan's access. Deleting the app also removes it, along with any blocks.

Your report

The report is blank or slow to appear

iOS prepares the report privately, and the first one can take up to 30 seconds. If it stays blank, tap Refresh. A new iPhone, or one with Screen Time recently turned on, may not have a week of history yet.

My numbers don't match Screen Time in Settings

ScreenSpan uses completed days only, so today isn't included until it's over. It counts iPhones, not iPads, and adds up time from every iPhone signed in to your Apple Account. Averages skip days with no reported activity.

Progress asks for a starting point

Screen Time keeps about a month of history. Once the week before you started is gone, Progress needs your starting daily average, which you can enter in Settings › Your progress.

ScreenSpan Pro

What does Pro include?

App blocking: focus blocks, schedules, daily limits and strict mode. The life chart, stats and progress are free.

Restoring a purchase

Open Settings in ScreenSpan and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancelling

Open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone and choose ScreenSpan Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Your blocking setup is kept if you subscribe again later.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Blocking

A block didn't start or end on time

iOS starts and stops blocks in the background, and occasionally runs late. Opening ScreenSpan brings every block back in line with its schedule.

I turned on strict mode and need out

Strict mode stops you from ending a block early inside ScreenSpan. It can't lock you out of your iPhone: removing ScreenSpan's access in Settings › Screen Time, or deleting the app, lifts every block.
