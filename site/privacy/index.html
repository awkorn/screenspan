Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

In short: ScreenSpan doesn't collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your Screen Time activity never leaves Apple's private Screen Time report on your iPhone, and what you type into the app stays on your device.

This policy explains how the ScreenSpan iPhone app and its extensions ("ScreenSpan", "we") handle information. It applies to the app and to this website.

Screen Time activity

With your permission, ScreenSpan uses Apple's Screen Time API (the FamilyControls, DeviceActivity and ManagedSettings frameworks) to show your phone use and to block apps you choose.

  • Your activity, such as daily totals, pickups and the apps you use most, is processed inside a Screen Time report extension that iOS runs in isolation. iOS doesn't let that data leave the extension, so ScreenSpan itself never receives it, and we never store or transmit it.
  • App names and icons are drawn by iOS from private tokens. ScreenSpan can't read which apps they are.
  • You can turn off ScreenSpan's Screen Time access at any time in your iPhone's Settings › Screen Time.

What the app stores on your iPhone

To work, ScreenSpan saves these settings in storage shared only between the app and its own extensions on your device:

  • Your age, worked out from the birth date you pick (the date itself isn't saved), and the planning age you choose.
  • Your daily screen time goal, your own estimate of your daily phone time, and the things you said you'd rather spend time on.
  • The date you started using ScreenSpan and, if you enter one, your starting daily average.
  • Your blocking setup: the apps you chose to block (as private Screen Time tokens), focus blocks, schedules, daily limits and strict mode.
  • Whether you have an active ScreenSpan Pro subscription.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Like other app data, it's included in your iPhone's own backups. Deleting ScreenSpan deletes it and removes any blocks it set.

Purchases

ScreenSpan Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app asks the App Store only whether your subscription is active. To manage or cancel it, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Network use

The app connects only to Apple's App Store, to load prices, make purchases and check your subscription. It contains no third-party code that collects data.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to run and protect the site.

Children

ScreenSpan isn't directed at children under 13, and the app asks for an age of at least 13. Because we don't collect personal data, we don't knowingly hold any from children.

Your choices and rights

Since we don't hold personal data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You control everything on your device: change your settings in the app, turn off Screen Time access in Settings, or delete the app to remove all of its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If a change means the app starts collecting data, we'll say so in the app before it happens.
